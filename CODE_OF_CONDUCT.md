# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the safety of users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal/political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing real guest health data in project channels.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate professionally.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
do not align with this Code of Conduct. Report conduct privately through the
maintainer contact options available on the GitHub repository.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters and apply
responses proportionately, up to temporary or permanent exclusion from project
spaces.
